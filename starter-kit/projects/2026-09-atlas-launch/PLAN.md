# Plan: Atlas 5.0 launch campaign

Status: **approved by Lukas, 2026-08-11**
Change this file only when the plan genuinely changes, and note why in PROGRESS.md.

---

## Phase 1 — Positioning and message
**Goal:** decide the one thing this launch is about.
**Inputs:** `BRIEF.md`, `knowledge/entities/products.md`, release notes from
Product, the last three customer calls.
**Outputs:** `research/positioning.md` with three candidate angles, the evidence
for each, and the objection each has to survive.
**Done when:** Lukas has chosen one angle and it is marked `CHOSEN` in the file.
👤 *Requires a human decision.*

## Phase 2 — Claims and approvals
**Goal:** know exactly what we are allowed to say.
**Inputs:** chosen angle, Product's release notes.
**Outputs:** a draft update to `knowledge/entities/products.md` listing 5.0
capabilities, what it explicitly does not do, and proposed approved claims.
**Done when:** the draft is sent to Legal. Nothing else starts until it's back.
👤 *Requires Legal.*

## Phase 3 — Channel plan and calendar
**Goal:** know what goes out, where, and when.
**Inputs:** approved claims, channel files.
**Outputs:** `CAMPAIGN.md` with a dated calendar, one row per asset, with owner
and dependency.
**Done when:** every asset has a date, an owner, and a channel.

## Phase 4 — Core assets
**Goal:** draft the announcement blog post and the customer email.
**Outputs:** `drafts/announcement-blog.md`, `drafts/customer-email.md`
**Done when:** both pass the self-review checklist and are in the drafts folder.

## Phase 5 — Supporting assets
**Goal:** everything else.
**Outputs:** two supporting blog posts, three newsletter issues, LinkedIn posts
for the company page and Jana, the sales one-pager.
**Done when:** all drafted, all in `drafts/`, all with open questions listed.
**Note:** Jana's posts need her story approved first — draft around the gap with
`[NEEDS STORY FROM JANA: ...]` if she's away.

## Phase 6 — Review pass
**Goal:** catch the problems before other people do.
**Outputs:** a review report — every unsourced claim, every rule breach, the
three weakest points a CFO would attack.
**Done when:** Lukas has read the report and decided what to fix.
👤 *Requires a human decision.*

## Phase 7 — Package and hand off
**Goal:** one page that anyone can act on.
**Outputs:** final `CAMPAIGN.md`, a list of everything still blocked on a human,
and who needs to do what by when.
**Done when:** Lukas can forward the file without explaining it.

---

## Assumptions
- Pricing is unchanged. **Not confirmed** — if it changes, Phases 2 and 4 redo.
- No webinar. If that changes, add a phase.
- Release notes arrive by 18 August. If later, everything shifts.
