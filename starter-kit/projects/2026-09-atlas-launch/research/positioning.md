# Positioning: Atlas 5.0

Phase 1 working file. Nothing here is approved and nothing here is a claim we
are allowed to make yet — Legal sees the claims in Phase 2.

## Angle A — "The upgrade your foremen won't notice"

**The promise:** 5.0 changes nothing about how the site team works. Same two
taps to capture, same offline behaviour. The change is entirely on the office
side.

**Evidence:**
- Three of the last three customer calls raised retraining as the reason they
  had not upgraded. Källström (12 sites) put it plainly: "I am not doing another
  Tuesday morning with forty people in a room."
- Support ticket volume after the 4.2 upgrade: 31 tickets in the first fortnight,
  27 of them from site users, none from the office.
  `[NEEDS SOURCE: pull the exact numbers from the support export before use]`

**The objection it has to survive:** if nothing changes for the site team, why
would anyone upgrade at all? This angle has to carry the office-side benefit in
the same breath, or it argues itself out of the launch.

---

## Angle B — "Everything you documented, finally searchable"

**The promise:** four years of site diaries, photos and RFIs stop being an
archive you cannot get anything out of.

**Evidence:**
- The single most requested thing in the 2026 customer survey, by a distance.
  `[NEEDS SOURCE: survey question number and n]`
- Two of the last three calls described the same workaround — exporting to PDF
  and searching in Preview.
- It is the one part of 5.0 with no equivalent in 4.x, so it is the only story
  that cannot be told about the version they already have.

**The objection it has to survive:** search quality is judged instantly and
harshly. If we lead with this, the demo has to be flawless on the first query,
on a real customer's data and not on ours.

## What any angle has to deal with

The 4.x → 5.0 upgrade is not automatic for self-hosted customers, which is
roughly 40% of the base. It was not in the brief and nobody had flagged it. Any
angle that reads as "just upgrade" will land badly with two fifths of the
audience, so the customer email needs an instruction section rather than an
announcement.
