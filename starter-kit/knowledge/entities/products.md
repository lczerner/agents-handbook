## Atlas

**One-liner:** Site documentation that works without signal.
**Launched:** March 2024 · **Current version:** 4.2 (June 2026)
**Price:** From €39/user/month, billed annually. Minimum 10 users.

### What it does
- Photo and note capture on site, offline, syncing when back in range
- RFI creation and tracking, with automatic reminders
- Daily site diary generated from the day's captures
- Export to PDF, and integrations with Procore and Autodesk Build

### What it does NOT do — do not imply otherwise
- No scheduling or Gantt functionality
- No cost or budget tracking
- No BIM model viewing
- Android and iOS only. There is **no desktop app** for site use.

### Approved claims
- "Works with no signal and syncs later." ✅
- "Average RFI turnaround across our customers dropped from 9 days to 3."
  ✅ — source: internal customer study, March 2026, n=41. Always say
  "across our customers", never "in the industry".
- "Set up in an afternoon." ✅ — median onboarding time is 3.5 hours.

### Claims we must NOT make
- ❌ Any ROI figure tied to a timeframe. Legal has said no.
- ❌ "Fastest", "most-used", "leading" — unsubstantiated.
- ❌ Any comparison naming a competitor.
- ❌ Anything about compliance with a specific regulation. Ask Legal every time.

## Beacon

**One-liner:** The office side of Atlas. Read-only dashboard for project managers.
**Launched:** November 2025 · **Price:** Included with Atlas, no separate licence.

### What it does
- Live view of what site teams captured today, across all active projects
- RFI status board with ageing
- Weekly digest email to nominated recipients

### What it does NOT do
- No editing. Everything originates on site in Atlas.
- No custom reporting or report builder. Fixed views only.
- No user-level permissions beyond admin/viewer.

### Approved claims
- "See what happened on every site this morning, before your first meeting." ✅

### Known objection and our honest answer
*"Can I build my own reports?"* — No, not yet. Say so plainly. It's the most
common request and pretending otherwise costs us trust in the first demo.
