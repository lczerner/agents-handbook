# Channel: Lighthouse Blog (lighthouse.com/blog)

Last verified: 2026-07-01

## Purpose
Organic search acquisition. Every post targets a keyword a construction ops
manager would actually type. This is not a company news channel.

## Reader
Ops manager at a 50–500 person construction firm. Arrives from Google with a
specific problem. Skims first, reads second. Often on a phone, on site, in poor
light, between other jobs.

## Format
- 1,200–1,800 words
- H2 every 250–350 words, sentence case
- **Answer the title question in the first 100 words.** Do not build up to it.
- At least one table or checklist
- Ends with a single CTA to the relevant product page

## Rules
- Target keyword in the title, the first paragraph, and one H2. Nowhere else forced.
- Meta description under 155 characters, written as a promise rather than a summary.
- Internal-link to 2–3 existing posts from `knowledge/library/articles/`
- Image briefs describe a real jobsite scene. No stock-photo clichés (no hard hats
  pointing at tablets, no handshakes, no glowing networks).

## Never on this channel
- Product announcements — those go to the newsletter
- First-person company voice ("we're excited to announce...")
- Listicles with more than seven items
- Anything that reads as a competitor comparison

## Good examples
- `library/articles/why-site-diaries-fail.md`
- `library/articles/rfi-turnaround-benchmarks.md`
