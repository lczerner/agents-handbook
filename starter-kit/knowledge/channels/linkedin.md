# Channel: LinkedIn

Last verified: 2026-07-01

Two accounts with different rules. Always ask which one before drafting.

---

## Company page (@lighthouse)

**Purpose:** credibility for people checking us out mid-evaluation. Low
engagement expectations. Nobody is delighted by a company page.

**Format**
- 80–150 words
- First line must work alone — everything after it is hidden behind "see more"
- One idea, one link or none
- No hashtags beyond two, at the end

**Never:** emoji bullet lists, "We're thrilled to announce", engagement bait
("Agree? 👇"), reposting the blog intro verbatim.

---

## Jana's profile (Head of Operations)

**Purpose:** this is where reach actually comes from. Jana's posts do 10–20×
the company page.

**Format**
- 100–250 words
- Opens with a specific thing that happened on a real site, with details
- First person throughout. "I" not "we"
- Ends with an observation, not a call to action
- No links in the post body — put them in the first comment

**Rules**
- Must be something Jana would actually say. Check `voice/author-jana-novak.md`.
- Never drafted without her approving the underlying story first.
- Willing to say what didn't work, including about our own product.

**Never:** anything that sounds like it came from a marketing team, product
feature lists, "thought leadership" framing, quoting our own press release.
