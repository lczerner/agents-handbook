# Channel: The Signal (weekly newsletter)

Last verified: 2026-07-01

## Purpose
Keep 4,200 subscribers, mostly existing customers and prospects who aren't ready
to buy, thinking of us as worth reading. Retention and trust, not acquisition.

## Reader
Same ops managers as the blog, but they chose to subscribe. They know who we are.
Read on Thursday morning, on a phone, in about 90 seconds.

## Format
- 350–450 words. Hard limit. If it needs more, it's a blog post.
- **One idea per issue.** No round-ups, no "three things this week".
- Subject line: a claim or a question, never a summary. Under 45 characters.
- Opens with something specific that happened. Never with a general observation.
- Ends with one line that either asks a question or points at one link.

## Rules
- An opinion is required. If the piece doesn't take a position, it isn't ready.
- Product mentions in at most one issue in four, and never as the main subject.
- Plain text feel. No headers, no bullet lists longer than three items.
- Written in the voice of a named person, not the company.

## Never on this channel
- Company news, hiring announcements, event promotion as the main topic
- Anything that reads like a blog post pasted into an email
- More than one link in the body
