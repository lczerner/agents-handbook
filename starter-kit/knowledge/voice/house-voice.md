# House voice

Last verified: 2026-07-01
Applies to everything we publish. Read this alongside the channel file.

## In one line
We sound like an experienced site manager explaining something to a colleague
over coffee. Direct, specific, slightly dry. Never a vendor.

## Rules
- Second person. "You", not "companies" or "organisations".
- Active voice. Name who does the thing.
- Sentences under 25 words. Vary the length or it reads like a manual.
- Concrete numbers over vague scale: "cut RFI turnaround from 9 days to 3",
  not "dramatically improves efficiency".
- One idea per paragraph. Three sentences maximum.
- British English.
- Contractions are fine and preferred. "Doesn't", not "does not".

## Do this, not that

❌ "Leveraging our innovative platform, teams can seamlessly streamline their
   documentation workflows."
✅ "Your site team files an RFI from their phone. The office sees it the same
   minute."

❌ "In today's fast-paced construction environment, staying ahead is more
   important than ever."
✅ "Most delays don't start on site. They start in an inbox."

❌ "Our solution empowers stakeholders to optimise project outcomes."
✅ "Foremen stop chasing paperwork. Project managers stop chasing foremen."

❌ "Digital transformation is reshaping the construction industry."
✅ "Half the sites we visit still run their diary on a WhatsApp group."

❌ "This comprehensive guide will explore the various considerations around
   documentation compliance."
✅ "Here's what an inspector actually asks for, and what happens if you can't
   produce it."

❌ "Many organisations struggle with visibility across their project portfolio."
✅ "You find out a job slipped when someone mentions it in a meeting."

❌ "Robust processes are essential for successful delivery."
✅ "A process nobody follows is worse than no process. At least then you know."

❌ "We're excited to share some thoughts on best practice."
✅ "We got this wrong for two years. Here's what changed."

❌ "It's not just about documentation, it's about accountability."
✅ "Documentation is only useful if someone is accountable for reading it."

❌ "Utilise the platform to facilitate improved communication."
✅ "Use it to stop the same question being asked four times."

## Banned words and phrases
leverage · seamless · game-changing · robust · delve · unlock · elevate ·
revolutionary · cutting-edge · best-in-class · journey (unless literal) ·
"in today's fast-paced world" · "it's not just X, it's Y" · "the truth is" ·
"let's dive in" · "at the end of the day"

## Punctuation
- No em dashes. Comma or full stop.
- No exclamation marks in body copy.
- Serial comma: no.
- Numbers: spell out one to nine, numerals from 10.
- Dates: 14 May 2026.

## The test
Read it aloud. If you wouldn't say it to a foreman standing in front of you,
rewrite it.
