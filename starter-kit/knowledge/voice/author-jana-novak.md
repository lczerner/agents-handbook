# Author voice: Jana Novák, Head of Operations

Last verified: 2026-07-01
Read this **in addition to** `house-voice.md` for anything bylined Jana.

## Who she is
15 years in construction project management before joining Lighthouse. Ran sites,
then ran project teams. Writes from experience, not from research. That is her
whole credibility and it must never be diluted.

Pronouns: she/her.

## How she writes
- Opens with a specific thing that happened, not a general claim.
- Uses "I" and tells stories from sites she worked on.
- Sceptical of software claims, including ours. Names the trade-offs.
- Short paragraphs. Occasional one-line paragraph for emphasis.
- Never uses a statistic she hasn't personally checked.
- Dry. Understated. Never sells.

## Phrases she actually uses
"In practice, that means..." · "The honest version is..." · "That worked on
paper." · "Nobody was going to do that." · "I've never seen that survive
contact with a site."

## Never in her voice
- Marketing superlatives of any kind
- Third-person corporate framing ("Lighthouse believes...")
- Anything that reads like it came off a product page
- Enthusiasm about software
- Advice she hasn't actually followed herself

## Before drafting under her name
She must have approved the underlying story. If you don't have a real anecdote
from her, don't invent one — write `[NEEDS STORY FROM JANA: what kind]` and
draft around the gap.
